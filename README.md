# HOUSE OF THE UNDEAD

A browser FPS roguelite where you fight casino zombies, earn chips, gamble between rounds, and unlock upgrades. Losing wagers adds penalties. Bosses appear on rounds 5 and 10. 

## Run

```powershell
cd HOUSE-OF-THE-UNDEAD
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Open `http://localhost:8000`.

## Controls

* WASD: Move
* Mouse: Aim and shoot
* Shift: Sprint
* Space: Jump
* Q: Dash
* G: Chip bomb
* F: Melee
* R: Reload
* 1–4: Change weapons
* Esc: Pause

## Main Features

* 10-round campaign and endless mode
* Daily seeded challenge
* Four weapons and multiple enemy types
* Blackjack, roulette, poker, and slots
* Weapon upgrades, cursed upgrades, and penalties
* Boss fights, special rounds, and optional objectives
* Permanent Vault progression
* Mid-run saves and local high scores
* Procedural music and 3D audio
* No internet required after loading

All chips and wagers are fictional and have no real-world value.
