HOW THE HOUSE PLAYS

A night in a casino that never closed. Ten rounds, two bosses, and every chip you win goes back on the felt.

  1. 🧟SURVIVE THE ROUNDThe dead gamblers walk in waves. Shoot them, pistol-whip the close ones, dash through a crowd. Clear the floor to end the round.
  2. 🪙EARN CHIPSEvery kill pays, and fast kills build a streak that pays more. Spend chips mid-round on cocktail perks, paid doors to new wings and the Big Six wheel.
  3. 🃏GAMBLE FOR UPGRADESBetween rounds the House Shop deals cards. Sit at blackjack, roulette or poker: win and pick an upgrade of the card's tier; lose and the next round gets a penalty. The slot machine pays out in chips.
  4. ☠BEAT THE HOUSEThe Pit Boss walks the floor on round 5 and the House Dealer deals on round 10. Beat the house to cash out, or stay for endless rounds.
🤝 WITH FRIENDSUp to four players. Host a table, send the invite link. Everyone gets their own shop. Hold E over a downed friend to revive them.
🎲 SEEDSEvery run is dealt from a seed. Type one in at the Coat Check to replay the exact same night, or share yours.
♣ GOING EASYSettings → ASSIST & ACCESS: half damage, gentler bets and a second chance, plus aim assist and reduce motion. Nothing is locked away for using it.
⌨ CONTROLSWASD move · Q dash · E use · R reload · G lethal · ESC pause. Rebind any key in Settings → Controls.

Every model, texture, sound and note is made by code as you play. Built on three.js and PeerJS (both MIT licensed). The band plays "When the Saints Go Marching In" (traditional) and two originals. All chips are fictional; no real money, ever.

THE VAULT

A cut of every run's chips is deposited here — 20%, plus 2% per round you reached, plus 10% for beating the house. Only chips you EARNED count; Front Money starting chips can't be re-deposited. Spend it on permanent perks.
