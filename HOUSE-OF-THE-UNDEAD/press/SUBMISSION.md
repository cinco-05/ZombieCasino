## c. Project description

**Short (about 60 words):**

> A 1930s rubber-hose cartoon shooter for one to four players, right in the
> browser. Fight waves of undead gamblers through a haunted Las Vegas casino,
> then bet the chips you earned at blackjack, roulette and five card draw to
> win upgrades, or pull the arm on a real three-reel slot machine. Lose a bet
> and the next round gets harder. Beat the House Dealer on round 10.

**Long:**

> **The casino never closed. The gamblers never left.**
>
> HOUSE OF THE UNDEAD is a first-person roguelite where gambling is the upgrade
> system. Each round, the undead regulars of a haunted 1930s Las Vegas casino
> come for you. Every kill pays chips, and fast kills build a streak that pays
> more. Between rounds the House Shop deals a fresh shelf of cards: sit down at
> blackjack (with splits), roulette or five card draw, played on 3D tables.
> Win and you choose an upgrade card that shows exactly what it does to your
> gun ("Hair Trigger: fire rate 1.50 → 1.68/s"). Lose and you carry a penalty
> into the next round. Or pull the arm on LUCKY UNDEAD, a one-armed bandit
> that pays out in chips. The Pit Boss walks the floor on round 5 and the
> House Dealer deals on round 10, and after that the floor stays open for
> endless rounds.
>
> **Multiplayer:** online co-op for up to four players. Host a table and send
> an invite link (or a five-letter room code). Everyone gets their own jacket
> color, their own chips and their own shop, dealt from the same seed. Downed
> teammates bleed out under a floating revive cross until someone holds E over
> them. Hold TAB for everyone's ping, kills, deaths, damage and chips.
>
> **Made entirely of code:** there is not a single image, model or audio file
> in the game. The 3D casino, the cartoon characters, their textures and
> animation, the sound effects and a live jazz band (bass, piano, drums and
> horns swinging through three tunes) are all generated while you play. Three
> art styles: 1930s cartoon, 1930s black and white, and modern neon.
>
> **Every night is a story:** each run ends with its own front page of THE
> EVENING GHOUL, a 1930s newspaper written from what actually happened ("ACE
> 42 SPLITS 3 TIMES, REGRETS NOTHING"). The neon title on the menu plays like
> a xylophone, the dead behind the menu talk back when you poke them, and the
> casino answers when you shoot it.
>
> **Built for everyone:** one click from the menu to the first fight, with
> tips that teach as you play. An assist mode (half damage, gentler bets, a
> once-a-run second chance), aim assist, reduce motion and reduced flashing,
> offered after a rough start. Fully rebindable controls, seeds to share,
> mid-run saves, a loyalty card with permanent perks and 23 markers to chase.
> All chips are fictional. No real money, ever.

## How a judge can try the multiplayer in one minute

1. Open the link in Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a computer (it needs a keyboard
   and mouse).
2. Click **PLAY WITH FRIENDS**, then **HOST A TABLE**, then **COPY INVITE
   LINK**.
3. Paste the link into a second browser window (or send it to a friend). It
   loads straight into the table.
4. Back in the first window, click **DEAL US IN**.
