# The non-gamer playtest

A real person who doesn't play games, a computer with a mouse, and twenty
minutes. The protocol: if they look confused or stressed in the first thirty
seconds, something needs fixing. Software can't run this for you; this sheet
makes it quick to run yourself.

## Before they sit down

1. Open https://cinco-05.github.io/zombiecasino/ in a **private window**, so
   they get the true first-time experience (no saves, every tip fresh).
2. Turn the sound up.
3. Say only: *"It's a game. Try it. Think out loud."* Then stop talking and
   don't touch the mouse.

## Watch the clock

| When | What should happen | Note what actually happened |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | They find **ENTER THE CASINO** and click it. The fight starts after a 3-2-1 | |
| 5–15 s | They click the screen if needed, look around with the mouse, and fire | |
| 15–30 s | A tip card explains moving and dashing. They kill something and see chips land | |
| 30 s–2 min | They walk up to something glowing and see the buy prompt | |
| End of round 1 | They understand the shop: buy a card, sit at a table, play | |
| First death | The newspaper makes them laugh; they click PLAY AGAIN | |
| Two early deaths | The summary offers ASSIST MODE. Do they take it? | |

## The questions afterwards

- What were you trying to do in the first minute?
- Was there a moment you didn't know what to press?
- What made you smile? What annoyed you?
- Would you play another round? Would you play it with a friend?

## Red flags (and what to try)

- **Stuck before the first shot:** the pointer may not be captured. They need
  to click in the game window once.
- **Overwhelmed by the shop:** the sub-line under THE HOUSE SHOP explains it.
  If they still freeze, the shop text needs work.
- **Dying in round 1 again and again:** that's what assist mode is for
  (Settings → ASSIST & ACCESS). After two early deaths the summary offers it.
- **Motion-sick:** Settings → ASSIST & ACCESS → Reduce motion.
- **Can't follow the action:** Aim assist in the same place, or a lower field
  of view under PICTURE & SOUND.

## Then try it with friends

Host a table, copy the invite link, and send it to a second person on another
computer. Watch how long it takes them to get from the link to the first
fight. (It should be one click, plus the host's DEAL US IN.)
